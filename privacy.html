The only things kept in your browser are described below: your light/dark choice (key theme) and, if you import one, your deck.

Your deck

On the Calculator and Combos pages you can import a .ydk deck file. The file is read in your browser only. It is never uploaded.

After an import, the site saves the result in your browser's local storage under the key dddreamer:your-deck: card names and how many copies of each, the file name and the import date. This stays on your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and is only used to show your deck on those pages. Nothing is saved until you import a file.

To delete it, press "Remove my deck" on those pages, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

YouTube

This site only links to videos on YouTube. While you are on this site, nothing is sent to YouTube or Google. If you follow a link, YouTube opens in a new tab and Google's privacy policy applies there.

Contact by e-mail

If you write to me, I receive your e-mail address, your message and whatever else you send. I use it only to reply, and I delete it once the matter is dealt with, unless I have to keep it by law. I do not pass it on. My mail is handled by Proton (Proton AG, Switzerland).

Your choices and rights

This site keeps almost no data about you. What it stores in your browser stays there, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

You can e-mail me to ask what I hold about you (this is only e-mail you have sent me), to have it corrected, or to have it deleted.

If you are in the EU or UK, you may also have rights under the GDPR. If you are a California resident, you may have rights under California privacy law. I will respond to such requests as far as the law requires.
